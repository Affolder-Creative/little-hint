# Keep price ranges separate from shopping offers

A manually entered price range describes one item or gift idea, including an idea covering different acceptable products. Store its lower and upper amounts separately, use the upper amount for new planning estimates, and preserve actual payments and existing plan snapshots. Automatic checks pause while a range is saved because a checked offer for one product cannot replace that broader estimate; individual shopping offers remain exact prices for the same product and variant as defined in ADR 0005.
