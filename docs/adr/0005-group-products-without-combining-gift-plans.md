# Group products without combining gift plans

A product group is a non-reservable container whose products retain ordinary item identities. This preserves existing reservations, purchases, discussions and settlement histories while allowing several givers to complete a set. Sharing and recipient context are inherited from the group; grouping never grants additional access.

Shopping offers belong to one product and variant. Their verification identities and histories remain separate, while subscribers follow the lowest fresh verified price across those offers. Different products in a group never participate in the same price comparison.
