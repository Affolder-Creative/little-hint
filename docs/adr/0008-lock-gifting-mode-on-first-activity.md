# Lock gifting mode on first gift-planning activity

Allow authorized personal-list editors to correct gifting mode until the first reservation, contribution offer or contribution, purchase, or discussion note. The lock is permanent even if that activity is cancelled, removed, or loses access. This supersedes only the first-access gifting-mode lock in [ADR 0002](0002-wish-list-co-management.md): reveal scheduling still locks on sharing or co-manager access, and also on first activity. A scheduled reveal date alone does not lock gifting mode. Existing locked lists and existing group events keep their promises during migration. The generic locked state can indirectly suggest that planning has begun; this accepted tradeoff permits early corrections without revealing the actor, item, activity type, count, or time.

Group owners and appointed event leaders control gifting mode for all participant lists. Activity on any linked list locks the entire event, including after that participant leaves. Wish edits, drafts, browsing, watches, private ideas and spending assignments do not count. Current recipient, profile-manager, and co-manager authority remains unchanged; administrative editing alone grants no policy authority. Surprise redaction and the historical discussion audiences in ADR 0003 remain intact, including existing scheduled-summary exceptions.

Persist locks inside the same serialized database transaction as activity and policy changes. Reservation, contribution-offer and linked-purchase submissions carry the gifting mode reviewed by their author; stale submissions fail without activity, preserve form entries and require explicit review. Discussion submissions retain their existing audience token. Schema 20 separates gifting and reveal locks and requires app and worker upgrades together. See [issue #301](https://github.com/Affolder-Creative/little-hint/issues/301).
