# Protect purchased gifts while retaining collection cleanup and admin recovery

Protect items and gift ideas while any purchased units remain, including individual products and their groups. Block ordinary individual archival and removal, plus list/event Trash and permanent deletion; allow whole-list/event archival so seasonal cleanup remains possible. Purchase corrections release protection, while receipt, refunds and scheduled summaries do not.

Hide protected actions from surprise recipients and accept their absence as an indirect purchase signal. Restrict purchase-dependent permissions to editors, and disclose explanations only when existing privacy rules allow the relevant purchases to be seen. This extends the accepted policy-lock signal in ADR 0008 without exposing buyers, counts or dates.

A separate app-admin override, limited to existing content authority under ADR 0004, handles abandoned or incorrect purchase records without falsifying purchase history. Require a fresh review, explicit confirmation and reason, with mutation and audit committed together. Preserve financial records and private lifecycle notices. Account closure remains governed by its separate review. See [issue #303](https://github.com/Affolder-Creative/little-hint/issues/303).
