# Little Hint

Little Hint organizes wishes and gift ideas for recipients.

## Language

**Recipient**:
A person an item or gift idea is intended for. Managing that person's profile or wish lists does not itself make the manager a recipient.

**Item**:
A wish saved on a wish list for its recipients.

**Gift idea**:
A suggestion saved for another person outside their wish list.

**Discussion**:
A conversation about an item or gift idea. Its notes may have different audiences because of visibility changes or a restricted exchange context.

**Mention**:
A tag identifying an account holder in a discussion they already have permission to read. A mention does not grant access.

**Wish-list co-manager**:
A user authorized by an account holder to help maintain that account holder's wish lists and wishes while the account holder retains ownership of their account. This role does not make the co-manager a gift recipient.

**Archived item**:
An item that is inactive because it or its wish list has been archived. Archiving retains the item for reference and possible restoration.

**Archive time**:
The time an item or gift idea was most recently archived individually.

**App admin**:
An account holder who administers an installation and maintains content they already have permission to access.

**Family admin**:
A current family-group member authorized to administer that group and maintain its accessible shared content.

**Primary owner**:
The family-group member responsible for ownership and its transfer. The primary owner is always a family admin.

**Shopping offer**:
A shop link for a particular product and variant, with its own price and verification time.

**Product group**:
Related products presented together under a shared name and gallery. Each product can be reserved and purchased separately.

**Price range**:
The lowest and highest listed price for one item or gift idea. A range can describe acceptable alternatives while the idea remains a single gift to reserve.
