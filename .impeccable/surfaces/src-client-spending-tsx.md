# Spending

Mode: Operate. Audience: friends and families recording gift costs and planning purchases with one trusted partner. Preserve the incumbent Little Hint visual system and the approved spending, sharing, and budget mockups.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Keep actual spending and outstanding estimates readable while preserving gift surprises.
OWN-WORLD: Existing ivory/umber themes, sienna actions, system sans text, open rows, and the existing app shell.
STORY: Choose whose spending and the recipient or occasion, read totals, record a purchase, and optionally set a budget.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Page heading, personal/shared view, purchase action, person/occasion filters, totals and an optional budget. Less common filters expand on demand.
FORM: Approved mockup extension of Buying; no identity change. Seed: none (established surface).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Only shared entries contribute to partner totals. Mark estimates and missing costs explicitly. Purchase forms show prospective sharing before saving. No generated mockup artwork ships as UI.

## Assignment and purchase refinement

Optional spending-list assignment stays visible in idea and purchase forms. Changes to an idea preview affected unassigned records before save. Purchased entries combine quantity status and individual spending records in one dialog. Unassigned review preserves scope and uses individual edits. Mobile menus avoid fixed navigation, recipient metadata aligns under names, and suggestion titles use one line. Preserve the existing typography, colors and spacing.

Review: domain privacy and transactional tests plus Chromium/WebKit browser workflows. Visual polish covers mobile and desktop, light and dark themes, fixed dialog controls and menu placement. Physical iPhone keyboard and safe-area behavior still requires device verification.

## Private gift-plan lifecycle

Buying retains source availability separately from purchase status. A selected plan opens above the existing status filters with a bounded prose measure and standard action controls. Contribution returns show sending and receipt as separate events and reuse existing forms and open rows. The recipient's management screens remain independent of hidden financial activity.

Validation: synthetic Chromium desktop/tablet/phone journeys in light and dark themes; return entry and counterparty receipt confirmation; responsive overflow checks; source-state and financial-privacy domain tests. One polish pass corrected control/prose spacing. Existing typography, colors, and navigation remain the visual authority.
