# Contributing

Issues, bug reports, and suggestions are welcome. Please omit real gift data,
email addresses, tokens, and installation secrets from reports.

Outside code contributions are not being accepted yet. Affolder Creative is
establishing contributor terms that preserve its ability to offer separate
commercial licenses. A public license or a DCO sign-off alone does not transfer
copyright or grant every right needed for commercial relicensing. This policy
also covers patches submitted through issues. Maintainer changes and automated
dependency updates remain part of normal maintenance.

Maintainers use PRs into `main`, with Conventional Commit titles such as
`fix: preserve gift drafts` or `feat: add reminders`. Squash merge the PR after
CI passes. Never merge the release PR until its proposed version and changelog
are ready. See [releasing](docs/RELEASING.md) and [licensing](docs/LICENSING.md).
